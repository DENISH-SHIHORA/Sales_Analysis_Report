# Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to analyze sales performance across different dimensions such as:

* Sales trends over time
* Regional performance
* Customer segments
* Product sub-categories
* Profit and discount analysis
* Geographic sales distribution

The dashboard helps businesses monitor KPIs, identify high-performing categories, and make data-driven decisions.

---

# Dashboard Preview

## Main Dashboard

The dashboard includes:

* KPI cards for Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Discount
* Sales breakdown by customer segment
* Sales trends over order dates
* Sales by sub-category
* Regional sales distribution
* Geographic visualization using maps

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Performance

* Total Sales reached **2.30M**.
* Total Profit generated was **286.40K**.
* Total Quantity sold was **38K**.
* Total Discount provided was **1.56K**.

### 2. Customer Segment Analysis

* Consumer segment contributes the highest share of sales.
* Corporate segment is the second-largest contributor.
* Home Office segment contributes the least sales.

### 3. Regional Analysis

* West region generated the highest sales.
* East and Central regions showed competitive performance.
* South region contributed the lowest sales share.

### 4. Product Sub-Category Analysis

Top-performing sub-categories include:

* Phones
* Chairs
* Storage
* Tables
* Binders

Low-performing categories:

* Labels
* Fasteners
* Envelopes

### 5. Time Series Analysis

* Sales fluctuate significantly over time.
* Several high sales spikes indicate seasonal or promotional demand.
* Sales growth trend becomes stronger towards later years.
